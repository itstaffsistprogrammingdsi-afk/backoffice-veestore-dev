# VeeStore Backoffice visual direction

Reading this as: a backoffice for store, warehouse, and ecommerce staff, with a fresh, upbeat operational language, ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

The VeeStore logo supplies blue, yellow, and mint. Clean white surfaces keep operational pages bright, while blue marks primary actions, current navigation, links, and keyboard focus. Pale blue supports table hovers and selected items without competing with dense data. Dark blue-gray text keeps the palette grounded, and status colors keep their operational meanings. Cards stay flat so borders and spacing define groups; assistant hero panels use soft mint in light mode and blue-gray in dark mode. Motion stays on short control feedback and loading, with reduced-motion support.

The layout keeps dense work views calm, then varies the composition where the content changes: filters above results, approvals beside the relevant queue, and real business metrics grouped by task. It avoids decorative numbers, badges, gradients, and illustration.
